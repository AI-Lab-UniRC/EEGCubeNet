14 subjects

7 classes: Elbow Extension (EE), Elbow Flexion (EF), Hand Close (HC), Hand Open (HO), Pronation (PR), Supination (SU), Resting (RE, non movement preparation)

For every class, the dataset includes 60 segments (trials) of EEG signals recorded during movement preparation (1 second preceding movement onset)

The original source of the raw dataset: "25. Upper limb movement decoding from EEG (001-2017)", available at the BNCI Horizon 2020 database(https://bnci-horizon-2020.eu/database/data-sets).
